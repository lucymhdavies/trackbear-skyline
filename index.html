FROM WORDS TO SOMETHING TANGIBLE

Your progress has a shape.

  1. 01

    Export from TrackBear

    Open your account menu at the top right of TrackBear and choose Account. Scroll to the bottom and click Export your data. Bring the downloaded ZIP here exactly as it is.

  2. 02

    Find your perspective

    Choose a project and year. Square-root celebrates consistency; raw words puts the big days in proportion.

  3. 03

    Make it real

    Download the same model you see here. Open the STL in your slicer, check the model, and choose your print settings.

Your writing stays with you.

Your export is read, processed and turned into an STL entirely on your device. This app does not upload your data, track your activity, or send your writing back to the internet. It keeps your import in memory only; refreshing the page clears it.

The website and its bundled assets still need to load from the host. This ChatGPT-hosted prototype also uses ChatGPT sign-in; those hosting and sign-in requests are separate from your locally processed export.
